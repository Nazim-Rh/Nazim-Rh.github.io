Research projects

Star formation in AGN host galaxies

Indian Institute of Astrophysics · Prof. C. S. Stalin · M.Sc. thesis, University of Calicut

A feeding black hole can switch off star formation in its host galaxy or trigger it, and which one happens is still unsettled. This project maps where young stars are forming in three nearby AGN host galaxies, using far- and near-ultraviolet images from AstroSat’s UVIT.

  • Data: UVIT FUV (F148W) and NUV (N263M) images of NGC 3486, NGC 7314 and NGC 5033
  • Sources found with CIAO wavdetect, then measured by segmentation-based photometry in photutils, keeping regions with SNR > 3 in both bands
  • Corrected for Galactic dust (CCM89) and for each galaxy’s own dust (Calzetti law, from the UV slope)
  • Star formation rate from FUV luminosity (Kennicutt & Evans); region ages from FUV−NUV colour with Starburst99 models (0.5 to 50 Myr)
  • Detection tested on simulated UVIT fields with injected sources: on a galaxy-like background wavdetect was 96.6% pure, against 26.4% for SExtractor (821 of its 1116 detections were false)
  • Output: maps and radial profiles of star formation rate surface density, dust attenuation and age

Light curve prediction for 4U 1630−472

URSC, ISRO · Dr. Anuj Nandi, Ankur Kushwaha

Black hole X-ray binaries brighten suddenly when their accretion disc becomes unstable. This project forecasts the daily X-ray flux of 4U 1630−472 and flags which phase of an outburst the source is in.

  • Data: MAXI daily light curve, 2–20 keV, MJD 55053 to 60796
  • Models: LSTM, XGBoost and k-nearest neighbours, trained on 10% to 90% of the data
  • LSTM had the lowest error for one-day-ahead forecasts at every training fraction
  • KNN was most stable for 10-day recursive forecasts (mean MAE 0.127)
  • A rule-based classifier labels the source quiescent, outburst possible, starting or ongoing

The CIR–σ relation in spiral galaxies

University of Calicut · Prof. C. D. Ravikumar

The central intensity ratio (CIR) is a photometric measure of how concentrated a galaxy’s light is at its centre. It tracks black hole mass in early-type galaxies, and this work tests it in spirals. Presented as posters at the 4th BINA Workshop and RAM-XI.

  • 887 spiral galaxies from SDSS DR16, with CIR measured by aperture photometry in SExtractor
  • CIR correlates with velocity dispersion (Spearman 0.658, p < 0.0001)
  • The correlation does not depend on galaxy brightness or size

Photometric redshifts with machine learning

University of Calicut · Prof. C. D. Ravikumar

Redshift tells us how far away a galaxy is, but spectra are expensive. This project predicts redshift from broad-band colours using random forests, and adds galaxy type and emission-line class as inputs.

  • Data: SDSS DR17, retrieved with SQL through CasJobs
  • Features: five magnitudes, ten colours, a relative distance, morphology, BPT class and radius ratio
  • RMSE of 0.019 on 10 test sets of 10,000 galaxies, against 0.023 in the earlier random forest work of Carliles et al.
  • Predicted redshifts for 100,000 galaxies with no spectrum

Software I’ve built

N-Light

Desktop app that wraps AstroSat LAXPC data reduction. It uses laxpcsoft and HEASoft’s lcurve to make a combined light curve plus per-GTI plots, with an interactive window to inspect and slice it.

github.com/Nazim-Rh/N-Light

Verdict

Desktop app for classifying detections as real, fake or uncertain by eye, using FITS cutouts centred on each source with adaptive scaling. It also includes a catalogue crossmatching tool.

github.com/Nazim-Rh/Verdict

NAJMA

Novel Astrophotometric Joint Mass Analyzer. A pipeline for source detection and for measuring star formation rates and stellar ages from Poisson data such as UV and X-ray images, with tools for alignment, combining, cropping and extinction.

github.com/Nazim-Rh/Najma
